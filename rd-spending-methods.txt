AGRICULTURAL R&D SPENDING: PUBLIC-SOURCE DATASET
Version: October 21, 2026. Amounts are million U.S. dollars.

Use each series for the population and accounting measure it actually covers.
The file contains separate datasets, not components of one national total.

RECOMMENDED USE

1. National public agricultural and food R&D history:
   Use ers_public_performers, total, 1970-2021. The two components distinguish
   USDA intramural performers from state universities/cooperating institutions.
   Both include funding from multiple sources. The university component does
   not measure state-government funding. Preserve ERS's published 2022-dollar
   series. Its USDA-only net intramural series also has a 2022 observation;
   the national total and university component remain unavailable in 2022.

2. More recent reported federal spending:
   Use federal_reported_composite, 2003-2024. It combines USDA gross cash R&D
   outlays, separate USDA R&D plant outlays, and non-USDA-funded university
   agricultural-sciences expenditures. It includes forestry research. It is
   a qualified composite, not a reconstruction of ERS or a comprehensive
   national total. NIFA is the published NCSES agency R&D outlay amount.

3. Recent expenditure detail across funding sources:
   Use herd_university_all_sources, 2010-2024, for HERD-reported university
   agricultural-sciences R&D. The six sources are federal government, state
   and local government, institution funds, business, nonprofits, and other.
   USDA and non-USDA are drilldowns of federal funding; do not add them again
   to their federal parent. The separate wide file rd-herd-all-sources.csv
   contains one row per year for this comparison.

4. State and all nongovernment project support:
   gateway_reported_support_diagnostic retains observed 2004-2021 aggregates.
   These reports have mixed activities and incomplete nonformula reporting
   in 2010-2014. They remain a coverage comparison. Missing later years do
   not mean zero spending. All nongovernment support includes industry,
   other nonfederal, and self-generated funds; industry is not added twice.

ACCOUNTING AND ADDITION RULES

Outlays are agency cash payments. Recipient expenditures record institutional
research costs and need not occur in the payment year. Budget authority and
obligations are different measures and are not included as spending here.
Gross agency outlays retain grants and cooperative agreements. Do not add
USDA-financed HERD or Gateway federal sources to those gross agency amounts.
Count research plant once, separately from research outlays.

HERD's published institutional totals include inter-university pass-through
duplication. This dataset preserves that survey convention. No field-specific
adjustment was inferred from national, all-field pass-through amounts.
Its "other sources" includes foreign governments and other universities.
Institution funds includes cost sharing and unrecovered indirect costs.
Consequently the four non-U.S.-government source columns should not be
described as an exact measure of private gifts or private-sector research.

Do not add the ERS total to HERD, the federal composite, or the Gateway
diagnostics. Within any dataset, add top-level components only; totals and
nested detail rows are not independent additions. HERD-reported university
spending is a narrower field/population than the ERS agricultural/food series.

PERIODS, PRICES AND BREAKS

USDA amounts use federal fiscal years. HERD institutions report their own
fiscal years. Keep the year labels supplied by each survey.

Nominal amounts are retained first. Common constant-2022 amounts use the
March 2025 NIH/BEA Biomedical Research and Development Price Index (BRDPI):
nominal million * 1858.2 / year index. The 2024 index is preliminary.
The retained common-index table begins in 1993; earlier common-price cells
remain blank. ERS's original published real values are separately available
throughout its history and are not silently relabeled as the newer vintage.

HERD series have definition breaks in 2010, 2016 and 2020. Do not describe
changes across these boundaries as funding changes alone. Before 2010, the
federal composite assigns university federal dollars without an agency code
to non-USDA. Both allocation endpoints are retained in the wide federal CSV;
they concern source assignment, not a confidence interval or a national bound.

NIFA's newer capacity-reporting schema merges self-generated, industry and
other nonfederal funds, and consolidates several federal source fields,
including other USDA. Its federal residual therefore cannot be treated as
non-USDA funding, and recent nongovernment subcategories cannot be spliced
to the older detail without a compatible crosswalk. Research and extension
capacity fields are separately identified. These rules do not establish the
financial definition of a historical REEIS funding-summary extract.

VALIDATION AND COVERAGE DECISION

A fresh ERS download on October 21, 2026 agrees with all 583 retained records.
HERD's 15 annual agricultural-sciences totals agree with the latest FY2024
published historical table. Its seven 2024 total/source values agree with
the independently published funding-source table. Federal agency worksheet
headers, first/final years, units and overlaps were checked separately.
The reproduced 2003-2024 federal composite matches the existing tracker.
Missing values, source estimates and reporting gaps are retained explicitly.

The latest checked public ERS national total still ends in 2021. FY2025 in
the checked Federal Funds release is preliminary, and no matching FY2025
HERD source was established. The release therefore ends the recent series
in 2024. No national 2022-2024 values have been imputed from a narrower series.
The directly shared author workbook is not an input to these public datasets.

Remaining improvements are a publicly documented, compatible national
recipient series after 2021; the historical REEIS financial-field definition
and research scope; and a field-specific treatment of university pass-throughs.
They would expand or harmonize coverage. They are not reasons to alter the
published NCSES outlay observations or prevent release under the stated scope.

FILES AND MACHINE-READABLE FIELDS

rd-spending-public-series.csv: all four separate datasets. dataset_id, series_id,
parent_id and row_role identify totals, top-level components, nested details
and diagnostics. nominal_usd_million is the observation before deflation.
constant_2022_usd_million uses the shared vintage; the separate
published_ERS_constant_2022_usd_million preserves ERS originals. An empty
amount means unavailable. observation_status, accounting_basis, scope, flags,
source_url and source_locator give the interpretation of each row.

rd-herd-all-sources.csv: one row per year, 2010-2024, with all six source
categories and the federal agency split. All-other-sources is retained apart
from business and nonprofits. The combined institution/business/nonprofit/
other column is a subtotal and is not an additional source.

rd-federal-spending-public.csv: one row per year, 2003-2024, with research
outlays, plant, non-USDA university expenditures and allocation endpoints.

public-source-manifest.json and the local audit folder retain checksums,
source-period mappings, raw-record anchors and reconciliation results.
