AGRICULTURAL R&D FUNDING AND PERFORMER EXPENDITURES — OCTOBER 21, 2026

Two distinct datasets are maintained. Funding describes the source of support.
Performer expenditure estimates describe the institutions doing the work.
Their totals differ in scope and financial conventions and are not additive.
The source options are alternatives, not components to add together.

FUNDING
Preferred construction: NCSES USDA agency R&D cash outlays plus separate USDA
research-plant outlays; REEIS NIFA/predecessor, other federal, state and all
nongovernment funds. Do not add REEIS Other USDA transfers or USDA Appropriation
again on top of gross agency outlays. Nongovernment includes industry,
institution-generated funds, trusts and other nonfederal sources; these are
funds at covered institutions, not total private-sector agricultural R&D.

Recovered CRIS reports supply earlier funder columns, through2001. Published
amounts are nominal thousands, converted to millions. CRIS CSREES-administered
figures are expenditure-report values; the later REEIS funding summaries can
combine formula expenditures and nonformula obligations. A source-basis break
is marked at2002. Pre1999 retained NSF agency inputs combine R&D and plant:
the chart shows their nonNIFA total as a combined category rather than invent
a separate facilities amount. The original agency detail remains in the source
audit. These inputs are retained transcriptions with an unknown source vintage,
not fresh official downloads.1999–2001 have separately identified R&D and plant.

2002–2006 REEIS live queries return HTTP500. Retained public REEIS extracts from
the Fuglie workbook are explicitly labeled older extracts. Archived public CRIS
reports corroborate nonNIFA funder categories within publication rounding;
their CSREES expenditure figures should not replace the REEIS NIFA funding
values without acknowledging the financial-basis difference.

2021 current LMD omits formula and nonNIFA coverage. Its $1,217.912441m of
nonformula/directed NIFA grants is retained in preference to older covered
values. Only missing formula $295.840201m is restored from an independently
verified July2025 archive. Missing other federal $503.278619m, state
$979.425702m and nongovernment $644.001432m use retained public extracts.
NIFA preferred total is $1,513.752642m; all-funder total $5,565.458395m.
The raw incomplete2021 export remains unchanged and quarantined as a total.

Alternatives replace NIFA with NCSES cash R&D outlays, or both NIFA and the
other-federal component with NCSES/HERD university agricultural sciences.
Other funders stay unchanged. Earlier alternative years are unavailable where
no compatible separate source exists; their missing values are not zero.

PERFORMER EXPENDITURE ESTIMATES
The calculated series follows Fuglie's performer construction: sum Total
Funding for State Agricultural Experiment Stations,1890 land-grant institutions,
veterinary schools, cooperative extension, other cooperating institutions and
forestry schools. Exclude private SBIR performers. Add gross nonNIFA USDA agency
R&D and plant outlays, less nonNIFA research grants/cooperative agreements
already counted at recipients. The transfer deduction is REEIS Other USDA,
not USDA Appropriation or all USDA funding. Subtract it once.

Current Organization Type All/All reports are independently downloaded for
2007–2024. Use their Total Funding column for performer categories; current
NIFA Administered Funding values are defective (zero in2022–2024 and AFRI-only
in2021) and are not used as NIFA totals.2002–2006 use retained raw organization
extracts with unknown vintage. ERS published history is retained through2001;
2002–2024 are author-calculated REEIS/NCSES estimates. They are an extension
of the method, not newly published ERS observations. Published ERS remains a
separate view through2021. Its USDA-only2022 observation is not a national total.

PRICES AND DISPLAY
Nominal millions are preserved in downloads. Common constant2022 figures use
March2025 NIH/BEA BRDPI;2024 price index is preliminary. The retained common
index begins1993. The earlier ERS history retains its published constant2022
values; later history is deflated using the common vintage. No constant-dollar
values are fabricated before the available common index. Do not interpret
definition, population or source changes as funding changes alone.
